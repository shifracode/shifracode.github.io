Loud Academy: academy management system

Completed · launching soon

A complete CRM + ERP for Loud Academy, an English language academy in Egypt. One system runs sales, student affairs, payments and attendance, with a dedicated dashboard for every role.

For owners: your numbers at a glance

Live dashboards that show how the business is doing, today, this week or this month.

Short demos with sample data. The system interface is in Arabic.

  • Owner dashboards & statistics

    Revenue, expenses and net profit for any period, where money comes from and goes, each salesperson's calls, conversions and target, and the full customer funnel.

  • Leads to subscriptions

    From the first inquiry to the placement test to enrollment, with monthly targets and commissions.

  • Subscriptions & payments

    Multi-level subscriptions, discounts and partial payments by cash, bank transfer, wallet or card, with printed receipts.

  • Refunds with approval

    Cancellations and refunds go through team-leader approval before any money leaves.

  • Attendance & grades

    Attendance for every class, absence limits with alerts, and group grades in one place.

  • Daily cash drawer

    Each employee sees the cash, transfers and refunds they handled today, reconciled automatically.

Screens shown with sample data, and phone numbers are hidden. The system interface is in Arabic. Tap any screen to view it full size.

Built with: Django · Django REST Framework · PostgreSQL
